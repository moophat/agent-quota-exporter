## Bugs and fixes

1. **Codex `model` label `unknown` after a start mid-file** (`internal/codex`). A file present at first collect or after a restart is read from its end, so its `turn_context` is not seen and later calls get `model="unknown"` until the next `turn_context`. Replay: 4 of 56 requests (input 5416, cache_read 308480, output 1105, reasoning 55). Totals are right, labels not. Fix: take the latest `turn_context` from the tail already read for quota, or persist the model per file.
2. **Router provider labels** (`internal/router`). Custom provider ids look like `anthropic-compatible-<uuid>` (53 chars) and `openai-compatible-chat-<uuid>`; the 48-character rule turns them into `other` and merges two providers (70 of 8286 requests, 2 of 8 connections). Fix: strip a trailing `-<uuid>` before the check.
3. **Memory.** 17.3 MB plateau against the 15 MB target; raise the target or reduce per-profile heap. Plan for 20 MB with 8 to 10 profiles.
4. **State file rewritten 14 times in one `--once`** (once per source). Cosmetic; write once at exit.
5. **agy PATH.** The client lives outside the exporter's default `PATH` (`/usr/local/bin:/usr/bin:/bin`) on the node tested; the unit or role must set `PATH`. The client run peaked at 208 MB RSS for 5 s.
6. **First-collect time for Codex**: 2 to 3.8 s per profile (tail scan of 20 files); steady state 3 to 21 ms. `--once` on 3 Codex profiles took 3.8 s. Note in docs.

## Page plan shapes (`cockpit/web/docs/plan-all-vendors.md`)

1. `credits {available, balance}` is per account in the plan but per `scope` in the exporter (`all`, `premium`); use `all`, `premium` has no windows.
2. `state: out_of_credits` has no source: every real account reports `has_credits:false`. It cannot be derived from `agent_quota_credits_available`; define a rule or drop the state.
3. `balance` is a string or null (null on one plan); do not show a number or money.
4. Window names differ by vendor: `5h` and `7d` (Codex), `5h` and `weekly` (agy); scopes `all` (Codex), `gemini` and `3p` (agy).
5. Codex windows disappear after `resets_at`; an idle account shows zero or one window plus a reading time. A missing window is normal, not "no data".
6. Account ids collide: Codex and opencode `account` defaults to the alias, and discovered profiles are all `root`. Use host plus profile, or require `account`.
7. Router `account` is `acct-xxxxxxxx` for 5 of 8 connections (names are emails). The page needs a mapping, or `account_label: email` (Tu's privacy choice).
8. Router `requests_24h` and `tokens_24h` undercount for the first 24 h after the first start (no replay).
9. opencode has no quota: keep it `not_measured`; its provider ids are the stored ones.
10. `observed_epoch` for router and opencode: use `agent_source_last_success_timestamp_seconds`.
